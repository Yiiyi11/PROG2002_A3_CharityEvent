How it works

Three steps from interest to impact

1

Find your event

Search by date, location, category or keyword. Results are filtered in MySQL through the /api/events/search endpoint.

2

Reserve your tickets

Choose how many tickets you need, enter your contact details and confirm. Every registration is recorded with a timestamp.

3

Show up and give back

Check the venue forecast, arrive on the day and know that the funds go straight to the charity partner running the event.
